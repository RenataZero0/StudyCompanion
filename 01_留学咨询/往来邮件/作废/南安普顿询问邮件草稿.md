## 二、邮件正文（英文，可直接发送）

**Subject:** NCUK International Foundation Year – MEng Electrical and Electronic Engineering, September 2028 entry

Dear Undergraduate Admissions Team,

I am a student at [Shanghai Qingteng Academy, China], currently preparing for the NCUK International Foundation Year (IFY). I intend to apply for **September 2028 entry** and would be grateful if you could confirm several points about admission to the **MEng Electrical and Electronic Engineering (2+2)** at the University of Southampton Malaysia, and to the equivalent programme at the UK campus.

My NCUK IFY subject modules are **Technical Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Physics**, plus the EAP module.

**1. Module structure – the "two mathematics" question.**
NCUK IFY requires three academic subject modules. If a student takes **Technical Mathematics and Further Mathematics**, are these counted as **two separate academic subject modules** towards the three-module requirement, or as a single mathematics subject?

**2. The 96-points requirement.**
Your published NCUK entry requirements state that applicants are "usually required to achieve a minimum of 96 points across subject modules". Please confirm whether 96 points is (a) the **total** across all three modules, or (b) the **minimum per module**. Please also confirm the tariff you apply — we understand A* = 56, A = 48, B = 40, C = 32, D = 24.

**3. Which campus.**
Does the same NCUK IFY requirement apply to the MEng Electrical and Electronic Engineering (2+2) at the **University of Southampton Malaysia** and to the equivalent programme at the **UK campus**?

**4. Progression from Malaysia to the UK.**
For the 2+2 structure, is there an additional academic threshold to progress from Malaysia to the UK for years 3 and 4 (for example, a minimum GPA or module average at the end of year 2)?

**5. BEng versus MEng.**
What is the difference in entry requirements between the BEng and the MEng in Electrical and Electronic Engineering? Is it possible to transfer from the BEng to the MEng, and if so, under what conditions?

**6. English language.**
If I achieve the required grade in the NCUK **EAP** module, is a separate IELTS certificate required? Please confirm the EAP grade you accept (we understand grade B is normally equivalent to IELTS 6.5) and whether any individual sub-skill minimum applies.

**7. Other engineering programmes.**
Could you also confirm the NCUK IFY entry requirements for the **MEng Mechanical Engineering (2+2)**, the **MEng Mechanical Engineering (4+0)** and the **MEng Aeronautics and Astronautics (2+2)**?

**8. Scholarships and application timing.**
Are there scholarships available specifically to NCUK applicants, or to international students at the Malaysia campus? When does the application window open and close for September 2028 entry?

I would also be grateful if you could tell me whether Malaysia-campus questions should be directed to admissions.malaysia@soton.ac.uk or to Mr Allan Gan.

Thank you very much for your time.

## 三、这封邮件为什么问这八个问题（发之前自己心里有数）

| # | 问题 | 为什么要问 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 两门数学算不算两门 | **最高优先级。** NCUK IFY 官方就是「3 门学术科目 + EAP」。若 Technical Maths + Further Maths 被算作**一门**，你就只有 2 门有效科目 → 直接触发蒙纳士「最少 3 门」的否决条款，南安的 96 分门槛也就无从谈起。开不了 CS 课之后，这是你唯一的结构性风险 |
| 2 | 96 分是总分还是每门 | 官方页面（`International Foundation Requirements`）只写「各科目模块通常要求达到最低 96 分」，**总分/每门没写清**。若为「每门 ≥96」= 每门 ≥C（32 points），若为「总分 ≥96」= 三门平均 C。二者难度差别巨大 |
| 3 | 马来校区与英国本部是否同门槛 | 你的 MEng EEE 是 **2+2**（前 2 年马来西亚、后 2 年英国）。两个校区若是两套要求，选校策略完全不同 |
| 4 | 2+2 的升学门槛 | **2+2 的隐藏风险**：马来段两年读完，若达不到升学均分，可能被留在马来校区读完（变成 4+0）。这直接决定你能不能拿到「后段在本部」的经历——而这是回击「分校是否被当水校」最有力的证据 |
| 5 | BEng vs MEng 差别 | BEng 3 年 / MEng 4 年。**4 年制 integrated master's 一次性给你硕士学位**，省掉一次硕士申请，且多一个实习暑假。若 MEng 门槛明显更高，要知道差多少 |
| 6 | EAP 能否替代雅思 | Southampton 官方接受 EAP，但**单项要求**（是否每项 ≥C/D）未在同一页写清，且**澳洲学生签证**另需英语证明（Monash 邮件就明确建议仍考雅思） |
| 7 | 其他三个工程专业门槛 | 机械 2+2、机械 4+0、航空航天 2+2。**4+0 机械工程是 UoSM 唯一全程在马来西亚的工程本科**；若你不想出国两次，这是唯一选项（但也不会有「赴本部」经历） |
| 8 | 奖学金与申请时间 | 马来校区学费约英国校区 **1/2–2/3**（工程类约 48,000–58,000 马币/年 ≈ 7.5–9 万人民币/年），且有 10–25% 国际生学费减免（本科最高 60%）。2028 年 9 月入学，申请通常在 **2027 年秋**开放 |

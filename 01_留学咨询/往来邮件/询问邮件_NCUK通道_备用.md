## 三、英文正文（可直接复制）

Dear NCUK Student Support Team,

I am a student at [Shanghai Ivy College], an NCUK Study Centre in Shanghai. I will begin the NCUK International Foundation Year in September 2027, with the subject modules **Technical Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Physics**.

I would be grateful for guidance on three points.

**1. How are multiple mathematics modules counted?**

The IFY requires students to study three academic subject modules. My combination includes two mathematics modules — Technical Mathematics and Further Mathematics. **Does NCUK's official guidance to its partner universities treat these as two separate academic subject modules, or as a single mathematics subject?** If universities may count them as one, I would be presenting only two recognised subjects, so I need to understand NCUK's position before I confirm my module choices.

**2. A written statement I can forward.**

If NCUK's position is that Technical Mathematics and Further Mathematics count as two separate academic subject modules, could you provide a short written statement to that effect that I can forward to universities' admissions offices?

**3. Progression restrictions for students entering from Year 11.**

NCUK's entry requirements document notes, for US High School Grade 11 entrants, that "a small number of universities may not consider applications from students entering the foundation from year 11". I will enter the IFY after completing Chinese Year 11, with an average of 70% or above. **Could you tell me which partner universities, if any, restrict progression for students entering the IFY from Chinese Year 11?** This materially affects where I can apply.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours sincerely,
Clement [Surname]
[your email] | [your phone]

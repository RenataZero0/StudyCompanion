## 一、为什么只问 3 个问题

**核心判断：在 2026 年（11 年级）问 2028 年入学的招生办，绝大多数问题的答案要么不存在，要么在官网。**

- **答案不存在**：2028 年学费未公布、2028 年申请截止日未公布、flex-semester 过渡期后的预计毕业时间未定。问了只会得到「以届时公布为准」。
- **答案在官网/Handbook**：Honours 是否内置、60 天工业实习、计算机先修、条件录取途径——这些自己查比问人快。
- **问多了有代价**：15 问的邮件最可能的结局是「只回 3–5 问」或「请参阅我们的网站」。一旦被当成"让招生办替我做功课"，整封信都会被降级处理。

**唯一例外**：有一个问题**决定你 2027 年怎么选课**，而这是你面前唯一有时间压力的决策——所以它必须现在问。这就是下面第 1 问。

**其余 11 问已移到第五节「第二批问题池」**，并标清了各自的自查方式或最佳提问时机。

## 三、英文正文（可直接复制）

Dear Ms Yang,

My name is Clement [Surname]. I am currently in Year 11 at [Shanghai Ivy College] in Shanghai, China, which is an NCUK Study Centre. I will begin the NCUK International Foundation Year (IFY) in September 2027 as a one-year programme, completing in June 2028. My three academic subject modules are **Technical Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Physics**, alongside the English for Academic Purposes (EAP) module. I hope to apply to UNSW for 2028 entry, for either **Engineering (Honours) (Electrical)** or **Science (Computer Science)**.

The NCUK Course Finder lists both programmes at NCUK IFY grades of **BBB** with an EAP requirement of **Overall B**. I have found almost everything else I need in the UNSW Handbook, so rather than asking a long list of questions I have narrowed this to the three points below, which I have not been able to resolve from the Handbook, the website or NCUK's published information.

**1. How does UNSW count my two mathematics modules?**

This is my most urgent question, because it determines the module choices I must confirm for my IFY programme.

The NCUK International Foundation Year requires students to study **three academic subject modules**. Two of mine are mathematics modules — Technical Mathematics and Further Mathematics. **Does UNSW treat Technical Mathematics and Further Mathematics as two separate academic subject modules, or as a single mathematics subject?** If they were treated as one subject, I would be presenting only two recognised academic subjects rather than three.

If UNSW treats them as two separate academic subject modules, would a written statement from NCUK confirming this be helpful or required?

**2. Is IELTS Academic accepted in place of EAP?**

Is BBB with EAP Overall B a **guaranteed minimum** for these two programmes, or **indicative only**? And are these figures expected to remain in place for 2028 entry? I would rather plan for a higher requirement now than discover later that the published grades are not a guarantee.

If it is easier, I am very happy to be referred to the relevant admissions team, a regional officer, or a specific Handbook page.

Thank you very much for your time and assistance.

## 六、三个你可能需要记住的事实（不进邮件）

1. **EAP 要 B，不是 C** —— UNSW 的 EAP 要求是 **Overall B**，即 NCUK 英语模块 **70–79%（≈雅思 6.5）**。你此前按 65% 规划，差一档。**英语目标应改为 75%+。** 这也正是第 2 问存在的意义。
2. **2028 年 9 月这个入学点不存在** —— UNSW 自 2028 年起改用 flex-semester，该学年只有两个开学点：**2028 年 8 月 14 日**（Semester 2）与 **2029 年 2 月下旬**（Semester 1）。而你的 IFY 成绩也约在 2028 年 8 月公布，正好压在 Semester 2 的截止线上。另注：澳洲 2 月/7 月开学与 6 月结业的预科生在时间上偏紧，英国 9 月底开学反而咬合得更好。
3. **中留服对 Honours 的认定**（中介转述，非中留服官方原文）：普通学士与荣誉学士**均认证为「本科学历 / 学士学位」，认证书不标注「荣誉」**。⇒ Honours 的价值不在国内简历上的"荣誉"二字，而在 ① 澳洲直博入场券 ② 英国 G5 / 美国藤校硕士加分 ③ 澳洲技术移民 2 年学习的 5 分。另：**Graduate Route 已立法收紧** —— gov.uk 原文 `2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026 / 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027`（HC 1333，2027-01-01 生效）。你 2031/2032 年毕业 → **只能拿 18 个月**。

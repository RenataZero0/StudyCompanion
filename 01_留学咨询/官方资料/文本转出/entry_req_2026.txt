This guide details the entry requirements for students applying to the NCUK International 
Foundation Year (IFY). Please note the additional requirements for progression to The University of 
Manchester, outlined here. 
Age Requirements: 
Applicants must normally be 17 years old when they start the IFY in standard or accelerated delivery 
modes (or for extended delivery modes, when they start the final year of the IFY).  
Exceptions can be made for students who have fully met NCUK’s qualification requirements but have 
documented reasons for not completing the required years of study, for example where in -country 
school systems lead to high school qualification completion in less than the usual number of study. 
Applicants who are 16 can be accepted but such students will have limited progression options. 
Entry to Medicine: 
NCUK offers a number of progression routes to medical courses. Students who wish to apply for these 
courses must have a science background in their qualification prior to IFY and have performed well 
in excess of the minimum entry requirements outlined below. 
Art & Design: 
Students wishing to study Art & Design on the International Foundation Year should have previous 
experience of studying Art & Design at High School. 
English Language Requirements: 
The English language entry requirements are outlined in detail in a table at the end of this document. 
Students must be able to show English language ability at IELTS 5.0 level or equivalent prior to entry. 
Students should present a form of English languag e certification as well as the academic 
qualifications outlined below. 
NCUK can arrange for students to take a Password test either at the Delivery Centre or at home, please 
contact centresupport@ncuk.ac.uk  
Use the University Course finder tool to help you find your dream university degree course here.

# Q3 — Do UK universities count A-level Mathematics and A-level Further Mathematics as TWO separate subjects for a "three A-levels" requirement?

调查日期：本次会话（2026 录取周期页面，多数为 2027 entry）
证据标签：官方 = 大学官网/官方域名；官方媒体 = 权威媒体；中介 = 留学中介/课程平台；网友 = 论坛/知乎等；未查到 = 未在来源中看到

> 结论先行：见文末「结论」。核心事实：**多数英国大学（含工科）把 A-level Mathematics 与 A-level Further Mathematics 计为两个独立科目**，同一份 offer 里可以同时要求 "A\* in Mathematics and A\* in Further Mathematics"；只在少数社科/数理经济类专业（LSE 为代表）存在把二者合并计算的限制，**未查到任何工科专业有此类限制**。

## 1. 通用惯例：官方来源证明"数学 + 进阶数学 = 两个独立 A-level"

### 1.1 University of Oxford — Mathematical Institute（数学系官方 Offer 页）
- **VERBATIM**：
  > "For Mathematics or joint honours at Oxford, A-level Mathematics is required, and we highly recommend A-level Further Maths."
  > "For Mathematics / Mathematics and Statistics, Mathematics and Philosophy, or Mathematics and Computer Science, the standard offer for students taking A-levels, if Further Mathematics is available, is A\*A\*A including A\* in Mathematics and A\* in Further Mathematics."
  > "Candidates who have chosen not to take Further Maths may apply, and may in exceptional circumstances be made an offer based on the mathematics that they are studying. In recent years we have admitted fewer than three such students per year."
- 解读：同一 offer 中同时要求 Mathematics 的 A\* 与 Further Mathematics 的 A\* ⇒ 官方明确把二者当成**两个不同科目/两门 A-level** 计数。
- URL: https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/study-here/undergraduate-study/our-offer
- EAP 要求：未查到（该页未列）
- 证据标签：官方

### 1.2 University of Cambridge — Engineering（工科，2027 entry）
- 课程：Engineering (BA Hons / MEng)
- **VERBATIM**：
  > "To apply to any of our Colleges for Engineering, you will need A levels/IB Higher Levels (or the equivalent) in: Mathematics; Physics; Further Mathematics to AS or A level if your school offers it."
  > "Colleges usually require A\* in Mathematics and/or Further Mathematics, and will often ask for A\*/7 in Physics or another science subject."
  > "If your school offers Further Mathematics to AS or A level, you should take it."
  - 最低 offer：A level A\*A\*A；IB 41–42 with 776 HL

- URL: https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/engineering-ba-hons-meng （/courses/engineering 会重定向到本页）

## 5. 结论

1. **通用惯例（有官方证据支持）：是的，绝大多数英国大学把 A-level Mathematics 与 A-level Further Mathematics 算作两门独立科目**，可直接同时满足"三门 A-level"要求中的两门。最直接的官方证据：
   - 剑桥**工程**："you will need A levels ... in: Mathematics; Physics; Further Mathematics to AS or A level if your school offers it." + "Colleges usually require A\* in Mathematics and/or Further Mathematics"（学生需同时具备数学与进阶数学）；
   - 牛津数学："A\*A\*A including A\* in Mathematics and A\* in Further Mathematics"（同一 offer 内两门分别给 A\*）；
   - KCL 数学："A\*AA — Must include grades A\*AA in Mathematics and Further Mathematics (in any order)"；
   - 华威**工程**："accept A2 Further Mathematics as a full A2 subject"（注：页面年份 2017）；
   - 曼彻斯特 EEE：把 Further Mathematics 列为数学之外可接受的独立科目（但与物理互为替代，未明说"算两门"）。
   ⇒ 对 NCUK IFY 学生而言，**Technical Maths 与 Further Mathematics 通常可被认作两门可计分科目**（前提是该大学承认 NCUK 的这两门模块，须逐校核对 NCUK 官方分数线表）。

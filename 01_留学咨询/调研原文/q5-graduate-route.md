# Q5 — 英国 Graduate Route（毕业生签证 / PSW）现行规则与改革风险

**调查日期：2026-10-05**（本文件所有"现行"表述均以此日期为准）
**任务**：(1) 本科毕业生与硕士毕业生的现行 Graduate Route 时长，须引 gov.uk 原文；(2) 2024–2026 政策争论全过程，精确区分"今日已生效"与"仅提议/咨询/已放弃"；(3) 最近一次官方声明的日期，并标注 2028 年毕业前规则可能变动的风险。

**标签约定**：`官方` = gov.uk / 英国议会 / 移民规则原文；`官方媒体` = 主流新闻；`中介` = 非官方的律所、专业机构、留学中介评论（本文件中凡属律所或专业组织评论的，均在括号内注明身份，便于区分于留学中介）；`网友` = 论坛/社交平台。**本文件不做任何数字推断——凡未在来源中亲眼读到的数值一律写 `未查到`。**

## 一、核心结论先行（TL;DR）

| 问题 | 答案 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 本科毕业生现行时长 | **按申请递交日分档**：2026-12-31（含）前递交 = 2 年；2027-01-01（含）起递交 = **18 个月** | [gov.uk/graduate-visa](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa) 官方 |
| 硕士毕业生现行时长 | **与本科完全相同**（规则不分本科/硕士，同为"All other qualifications"档） | [Appendix Graduate GR 8.1](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-graduate) 官方 |
| 博士 | 3 年，**不受本次削减影响** | 同上 |
| 18 个月削减的性质 | **已立法生效**（已写入移民规则，属"已laid in Parliament"的成文法条款），只是生效日定在 2027-01-01 | HC 1333（2025-10-14）官方 |
| 2028 年毕业的该学生适用 | **18 个月**（其递交日必然在 2027-01-01 之后） | 逻辑必然 + 中介来源佐证 |
| 判定的关键锚点 | **申请递交日，而非毕业日、而非入学日** | 移民规则 GR 8.1 官方 |

> **对本项目学生最重要的一句话**：2028 年毕业 → 递交日必然晚于 2027-01-01 → **只能拿 18 个月，不是 2 年**。
> 而"保住 2 年"的唯一可能是 **在 2026 年 12 月 31 日前完成递交**，这与 2028 年毕业的学生**在时间上不可能兼容**。

### 2.2 本科与硕士是否不同？——官方页面对照

**URL**：https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa/course-you-studied
**标签**：`官方`

逐字引用：

> "You usually must have one of the following to apply:
>
> - a UK bachelor's degree
> - a UK master's degree
> - a UK PhD or doctorate"

**判定**：gov.uk 的"课程"页面把本科、硕士、博士并列为三种合格资格，**但完全没有按本科/硕士区分类别给出不同时长**；时长一律在"Overview → How long you can stay"中按**申请递交日期**分档。因此 **本科毕业生与硕士毕业生的现行时长完全一致**。

### 2.3 最具法律效力的原文：移民规则 Appendix Graduate GR 8.1

**URL**：https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-graduate
**标签**：`官方`（这是成文法条文本身，比 gov.uk 面向公众的说明页位阶更高）
**页面标注**：`Updated: 3 August 2026`

逐字引用（Period of grant for a Graduate）：

> "**GR 8.1.** The applicant will be granted the period of permission as set out in the table below:
>
> | Type of Qualification | Period granted from date of decision |
> | --- | --- |
> | PhD or other doctoral qualification | 3 years |
> | All other qualifications | **2 years** if the applicant has successfully completed a course at UK bachelor's degree or master's degree level, or another relevant qualification that meets the requirement of ST8.1/2/3 of the Rules, and **the application is made before 1 January 2027**. |
> | All other qualifications | **18 months** if the applicant has successfully completed a course at UK bachelor's degree or master's degree level, or another relevant qualification that meets the requirement of ST8.1/2/3 of the Rules, and **the application is made on or after 1 January 2027**. |"

**关键法律文本细节（极重要）**：
- 规则条文明确写 `bachelor's degree or master's degree level` **并列在同一行**，二者适用**同一条款、同一时长**。这是"本科=硕士"最硬的法律证据。
- 分档标准用的是 `the application is made`（**申请被递交**），不是毕业日、不是课程结束日、不是学位授予日。
- 时长从 `date of decision`（决定之日）起算。

逐字引用（其他相关条款）：

> "The Graduate route is an unsponsored route."

> "The Graduate route is **not a route to settlement**."

> "GR 8.2. The grant will be subject to all the following conditions:
> (a) no access to public funds; and
> (b) work (including self-employment and voluntary work) is permitted, apart from work as a professional sportsperson; and
> (c) study is permitted, except study with an education provider which is a Student sponsor...; and
> (d) study is subject to the ATAS condition in Appendix ATAS."

> "GR 1.4. The applicant must **not have been previously granted permission** under the Doctorate Extension Scheme or as a Graduate."

> "GR 6.1. ... | Total length of course | Relevant period of Student permission granted during which all study took place in the UK ... | 12 months or less | Full duration of course | Longer than 12 months | At least 12 months |"

**由此可确认的两个常被中介说错的点**：
1. Graduate Route **一生只能申请一次**（GR 1.4），**不可延期**（GR 8.2 无延期条款，gov.uk 明写 "You cannot extend your Graduate visa"）。
2. Graduate Route **本身不通往永居**（"The Graduate route is not a route to settlement"）——但居留时间可计入其他永居路径（见第四节 earned settlement 风险）。

### 3.9 平行战线：Earned Settlement（对 2028 年后长期规划的额外风险）

这不是 Graduate Route 时长的改动，但**直接影响该学生"留学 → 工作 → 永居"的整条路径**，属于必须提示的风险。

**白皮书原文（官方，2025-05-12）**：
> "Double the standard qualifying period for settlement to 10 years."

**进展（来源：EIN 特邀博客，作者 Danielle Cohen，移民律师，2026-09-10；标签 `中介`（律所/专业博客，非官方））**
**URL**：https://www.ein.org.uk/blog/what-earned-settlement

逐字引用：

> "On **20 November 2025**, the government published, *A Fairer Pathway to Settlement*, setting out the planned changes to the settlement rules. The statement was accompanied by a consultation on the changes, **which closed in February 2026**. The government has **not yet published its response to the consultation, which received over 200,000 responses**."

> "Under the proposals, the existing settlement rules will be replaced by an 'earned settlement' model. The **standard qualifying period will increase from 5 to 10 years** before people can apply for settlement, otherwise known as indefinite leave to remain."

> "The proposed mandatory requirements that all applicants will need to meet are:
>
> - The application must not fall for refusal under Part Suitability ...
> - The applicant has no current litigation, NHS, tax or other government debt.
> - The level of English language required is increasing to level **B2** of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages.
> - The Applicant must pass the Life in the UK test.
> - The Applicant must have **annual earnings above £12,570 for a minimum of 3 to 5 years**, with the exact length of time subject to consultation"

> "### What about the 10-year long residence route?
>
> The existing 10-year long residence route will be **abolished** and replaced with the earned settlement model. In the proposals, the government has indicated that only routes which lead to settlement will count towards the qualifying period. **This could mean that, for example, time spent as a student or a graduate will no longer count in the same way that it currently does under the 10-year long residence route.**"

> "### When will the changes be introduced?
>
> So far, **only the change to the level of English language required has been introduced**. From **26 March 2027**, those applying for indefinite leave to remain will need to pass an English language test at level B2 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages."

> "In March this year, **the Home Secretary stated in an interview with The Times that the earned settlement changes will be introduced in autumn this year**. We are waiting for further details from the government about when the new system will be introduced and how exactly it will work."

同一来源的"减免"机制（逐字，供了解，**不构成对学生的建议**）：

> "| Attribute | Adjustment to the 10-year qualifying period |
> | Applicant has competency in English language at C1 Level under the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages | Minus 1 year |
> | Applicant has earned a taxable income of £50,270 for 3 years immediately prior to applying for settlement | Minus 5 years |

**这一节对学生的含义**：Graduate Route 本身不通永居，学生若要长留英国必须转 Skilled Worker 等担保路线；而 earned settlement 改革正把永居门槛从 5 年拉向 10 年基线，且**"学生/毕业生身份期间的时间可能不再计入"**。这是与 18 个月削减叠加的第二重风险。

### 5.2 风险标注：2028 年毕业前的规则变动风险

**该学生的时间线（推定）**：NCUK IFY → 英国本科（3 年）→ 约 **2028 年毕业** → 申请 Graduate Route。

**风险等级：高（时长已被锁定为 18 个月）；在当前政治环境下"进一步缩短"为中等风险；"恢复 2 年"为低风险。**

具体风险点：

1. **【已确定的不利结果，非风险而是事实】** 学生 2028 年毕业 → 递交日 **必然 ≥ 2027-01-01** → **只能获批 18 个月**。这不是"可能变"，而是"已经这样了"。**规划时必须按 18 个月做预算，不要按 2 年。**

2. **【可能的进一步削减】** 白皮书用词是 "reduce ... to a period of 18 months"，措辞为**确定性承诺**而非上限。若移民数字政治压力持续（白皮书同时把永居基准期翻倍至 10 年、取消社会护理海外招聘等，显示政府在该领域持续收紧），**进一步缩短或附加条件（如限定高技能岗位、限定特定行业）在 2028 年前并非不可能**。本次**未查到任何已提出的"进一步削减到 18 个月以下"的正式提案**（见第七节 未查到）。

3. **【唯一的反向可能，但极弱】** 反对请愿 767146 截至 2026-10-05 仅 **37 签名**，且 **2026-11-11 即截止**，几乎不可能达到 10,000 的政府回应门槛。**本次未查到任何议员动议、上议院修正案或法院挑战在实质威胁这项政策。** 因此"恢复 2 年"的可能性评估为**低**。

4. **【真正的第二重风险：永居路径】** Graduate Route **不是**永居路线（规则原文）。学生若想长留，必须转 Skilled Worker 等担保路线。而 earned settlement 改革（咨询 2026-02 关闭，政府回应未出）拟把永居基准期从 5 年改为 10 年，并**可能使"学生/毕业生"期间的时间不再计入**。这意味着：**即使 18 个月顺利拿到，后续的永居路径在 2028 年前也可能显著变难。** 政府预计"今秋"（2026 年秋）推出细节——**这正好是本文件撰写期间，值得持续跟踪**。

## 六、结论（直接回答任务问题）

**（1）现行时长**
英国 Graduate Route 对**本科毕业生**与**硕士毕业生**的时长**完全相同**——移民规则把他们并列在同一档（"All other qualifications"），不做区分。
- **2026-12-31（含）之前递交申请：2 年**
- **2027-01-01（含）之后递交申请：18 个月**
- 博士及博士类资格：**3 年**（本次未受影响）
gov.uk 官方原文（https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa）："2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026 / 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027"；移民规则 Appendix Graduate GR 8.1 表格同义。

**（2）2024–2026 政策争论的结局**
- **2024 年**：内政大臣委托 MAC 快速审查；MAC 于 2024-05-14 报告，**建议保留 Graduate Route 现行形式**、明确未发现系统性滥用、警告限制会"过度纠正"；政府于 2024-11-01 正式回应"**接受 MAC 的建议**"，当时官方口径仍是 **2 年（博士 3 年）**。**"彻底取消 Graduate Route"这一选项至此被放弃。**
- **2025 年**：风向反转。2025-05-12《移民白皮书》（CP 1326）官方新闻稿明写"**We will reduce the ability for graduates to remain in the UK after their studies to a period of 18 months**"。**2025-10-14，HC 1333 号《移民规则变更声明》呈递议会，把 18 个月正式写入移民规则**，适用对象为 2027-01-01 起递交的申请人；博士维持 3 年。
- **2026 年**：2026-07-09 的 HC 259 号变更声明**只修改了在英出生子女的受养资格，未触碰时长**——说明 18 个月政策稳定、未被重新讨论。反对请愿 767146（2026-05-11 开启）**仅 37 签名，无政府回应**。

**（3）最近官方声明与风险**
最近可确证的官方日期为 **2026-08-03**（Appendix Graduate 页面版本日）与 **2026-07-09**（HC 259 呈递议会日）；本次对 gov.uk 现行页面的抓取日为 **2026-10-05**。
**风险标注：对 2028 年毕业的学生，"2 年"已经不可得——其申请递交日必然晚于 2027-01-01，只能获批 18 个月。这是既成事实而非风险。** 真正的风险有两层：①**进一步收紧**（白皮书措辞为确定性方向，若移民压力持续，附加条件或再缩短在 2028 年前不能排除；但本次未查到任何"降至 18 个月以下"的正式提案）；②**永居路径收紧**（earned settlement 拟把永居基准期由 5 年改为 10 年，并可能不再计入学生/毕业生期间的时间，政府称细节"今秋"公布）。**规划建议：按 18 个月做财务与求职预算，并把"毕业前 9–12 个月启动求职、争取带担保资格（Skilled Worker sponsor licence）的岗位"作为核心策略，而不是依赖 Graduate Route 的长度。**
